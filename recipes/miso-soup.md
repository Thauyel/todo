A simple, honest bowl. Not the restaurant version — the Tuesday-night version.

Serves 1 hungry person, scales linearly.

## Ingredients
- 400 ml water (or dashi if you're serious)
- 2 tbsp white miso paste (shiro miso)
- 80 g silken tofu, cubed
- 2 tbsp dried wakame seaweed (rehydrates in the hot broth)
- 2 spring onions, thinly sliced (whites and greens)
- 1 tsp soy sauce (optional)

## Method
1. Bring the water to a gentle simmer in a small pot. Don't boil hard — miso is delicate.
2. Meanwhile, put the miso paste in a small bowl or ladle. Add a splash of the hot water and whisk with chopsticks or a small whisk until smooth. This prevents clumps.
3. Add the tofu and wakame to the pot. Simmer 1 minute.
4. Turn the heat off. Wait 30 seconds (so the broth is just below boiling — boiling kills the miso's beneficial cultures and flavor).
5. Stir in the dissolved miso. Taste. Add soy sauce if it needs more salt.
6. Pour into a bowl. Scatter the spring onions on top.

## Notes
- Add a swirl of sesame oil at the end for richness.
- A handful of cooked rice dropped in = instant comfort food.
- If you have leftover cooked salmon or a soft-boiled egg, half goes in here.
