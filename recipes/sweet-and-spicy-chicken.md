Sticky-sweet chili-garlic chicken. Crispy on the outside, juicy inside. ~30 min total.
Serves 4 over rice.

## Ingredients
- 700 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into 3 cm chunks
- 2 large eggs
- 4 tbsp cornstarch
- 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp white pepper
- neutral oil, for frying (about 3 cm depth in the pan)

### Sauce
- 6 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2-3 dried red chilies, broken (adjust to taste)
- 3 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tbsp sambal oelek or other chili paste
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 scallions, sliced thin, to finish
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds, to finish

## Method
1. Pat the chicken very dry. Toss with salt and white pepper, then the eggs, then the cornstarch and flour until every piece is coated in a shaggy paste. Rest 10 minutes.
2. Heat 3 cm of neutral oil in a heavy pan to 175 °C. Fry the chicken in batches, without crowding, 4-5 minutes until deep golden and cooked through. Drain on a rack, not paper towels.
3. While the chicken fries, whisk everything in the "Sauce" list except the scallions and sesame seeds in a small bowl.
4. Pour off all but 1 tbsp of frying oil. Over medium heat, add the garlic, ginger, and dried chilies; stir 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the sauce — it will bubble hard. Simmer 60-90 seconds until glossy and syrupy.
5. Return the chicken to the pan and toss for 30 seconds until every piece is lacquered.
6. Hit with the scallions and sesame seeds. Serve immediately over steamed rice.

## Notes
- **Crispier finish:** after the first fry, let the chicken rest 5 minutes, then fry a second time at 190 °C for 60-90 seconds. Toss in sauce right after.
- **Less heat:** drop the dried chilies to 1 or skip them; lean on the sambal.
- **Sweeter:** bump the honey to 4 tbsp and the vinegar to 1.5 tbsp.
- **Chicken alternative:** works the same with boneless breast (slice thinner, 2 cm).
