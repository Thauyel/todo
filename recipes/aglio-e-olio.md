The original 5-ingredient weeknight dish. Done in 15 minutes, deeply
satisfying, infinitely variable.

Serves 2 generously.

## Ingredients
- 200 g spaghetti (or any long pasta)
- 4 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (more if you like heat)
- 30 g flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- Fine sea salt
- Zest of 1/2 lemon (optional, but it lifts the whole thing)
- 30 g grated parmesan or pecorino, plus more at the table

## Method
1. Bring a large pot of generously salted water to a boil. It should taste like the sea.
2. While it heats, warm the olive oil in a wide pan over medium-low. Add the garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook gently until the garlic is pale gold and fragrant — about 3 minutes. Don't let it brown.
3. Drop the pasta in. Cook one minute shy of the package time.
4. Reserve a mug of pasta water. Drain the pasta (or lift it straight into the garlic pan with tongs).
5. Add the pasta to the garlic oil with a splash of pasta water. Toss vigorously over medium heat until the liquid emulsifies into a silky sauce that coats the noodles, about 1 minute.
6. Off the heat: stir in the parsley, lemon zest, and cheese. Taste for salt. Add more pasta water if it needs loosening.
7. Plate immediately. More cheese at the table. Black pepper.

## Notes
- The magic is in the emulsion — the starch from the pasta water + oil + cheese becomes a sauce. Don't rush the toss.
- No cheese if you want to keep it traditional Neapolitan-style. (Parmigiano in this dish is a Northern Italian heresy. It's still good.)
- Leftover bread toasted in the garlic oil = a free second course.
